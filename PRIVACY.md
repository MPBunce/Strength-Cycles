# Forge Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 3, 2026_

**Forge doesn't collect, store, sell or share any personal information.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer can't see anything you do in Forge.

## What stays on your iPhone

Everything you log in Forge is stored only on your device: training cycles and sets, running plans, runs and races, daily work, stretching, challenges, goals and settings. Forge's Home Screen widgets read a summary of this from the same device. If you delete Forge, all of this is deleted with it.

## Apple services Forge uses

- **Apple Health.** With your permission, Forge reads your **step count** to show progress toward your daily step goal. It reads nothing else from Health, never writes to Health, and never sends your steps anywhere. You can turn this off at any time in the Health app under Sharing › Apps › Forge.
- **In-app purchases.** Forge Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and Forge only learns whether Plus has been bought.

Apple's handling of this information is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

Forge doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
